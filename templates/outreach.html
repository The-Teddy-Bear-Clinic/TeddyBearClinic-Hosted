Community Outreach Events

The Teddy Bear Clinic proudly participates in community outreach events that help families connect with local first responders in a fun and welcoming environment. Events such as Cone with a Cop give children the opportunity to meet police officers and firefighters, ask questions, explore emergency vehicles, and build positive relationships with community helpers. Check the Upcoming Events tab for upcoming event dates, times, and locations, as well as future opportunities to engage with our local police and fire departments.

Cone with a Cop — March 11, 2026

On March 11, 2026, the Teddy Bear Clinic successfully hosted “Cone with a Cop” at Denville Dairy. From 3:30 to 4:30 PM, over 110 local children gathered to meet eight of Denville’s police officers in a relaxed, friendly environment. The event, which aimed to foster positive relationships between youth and law enforcement, featured a temporary tattoo station, Denville PD bracelets, coloring books, and a special appearance by Mac, the Denville PD K-9 therapy dog.

While the children enjoyed free ice cream cones, they also had the opportunity to ask questions and engage directly with the officers. The event was covered by The Denville Edit, reaching their social media following of over 20,000. The initiative was a resounding success, driven by the leadership of students Francesca Piccolo, Duncan Felch, Gabriella Goodman, Carina Lu, Annaliese Monaco, and Cate Jacob, under the guidance of their advisor, Ms. Samantha Shane. The team also used the platform to distribute flyers for their upcoming event in May, ensuring continued momentum for the Clinic’s community outreach.
