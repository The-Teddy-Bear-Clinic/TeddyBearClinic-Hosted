Expansion

Bringing the Teddy Bear Clinic to new communities

Growing Beyond Morris County

What started as a student project at the Morris County School of Technology has grown into a model that other communities want to bring home. We are working with university and county partners to run Teddy Bear Clinics beyond Morris County, and to put our work into the hands of families through published children's books.

Expansion Clinics

University of Michigan

Fall 2026
Our first expansion clinic outside New Jersey, bringing the Teddy Bear Clinic model to the University of Michigan community.

Middlesex County

Spring 2027
Expanding across New Jersey in partnership with county vocational-technical schools.

More to Come

We are in conversation with additional county technical schools about bringing the clinic to their communities.

Our First Book

Teddy and the Fearless Firefighters

Written and illustrated by The Academy for Education and Learning at MCST

Uh oh! When Teddy's apple pie turns into smoke, can this little bear face the heat? When the alarm sounds, Teddy doesn't panic but remembers his safety rules! Follow along as the Fearless Firefighters arrive in their big red truck. You will meet the brave Captain Callie and learn all about essential fire safety words like hose, ladder, and the importance of a safe meeting place. This action-packed story shows kids that firefighters are friendly protectors whose gear is designed to keep them safe, not scare us away.
